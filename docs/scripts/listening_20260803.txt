まずは、遅刻の謝罪表現です。

Sorry, I totally forgot I had something to do today, so I'm running late.

I'm really sorry for being late — I completely forgot I had something scheduled today.

ここがポイントです。

カジュアルな言い方では、理由を先に言ってから遅れていることを伝えますが、フォーマルな言い方では先に謝罪してから理由を説明する傾向があります。状況に応じて使い分けましょう。

I'm really sorry for being late — I completely forgot I had something scheduled today.

Sorry, I totally spaced that I had something going on today, that's why I'm late.

次は、挨拶の表現です。

Hey, how's it going?

Hello, how are you doing today?

ここがポイントです。

"How's it going?" はとてもカジュアルな挨拶で友人同士で使い、"How are you doing today?" はより丁寧で、初対面やビジネスの場面にも使えます。

Hello, how are you doing today?

Hey, what's up?

As a result, the research I'll be introducing next is completely different in content.

ここがポイントです。

カジュアルな表現では "kind of a weird curriculum" のように曖昧でくだけた言い回しを使いますが、フォーマルな表現では "a somewhat unusual curriculum" のように、より客観的で丁寧な語彙を選びます。研究発表など改まった場面ではフォーマル寄りの表現を意識しましょう。

This is the content of my graduation thesis. I should mention in advance that our faculty follows a somewhat unusual curriculum, which means students cannot remain in the same lab from their undergraduate studies into graduate school. As a result, the research I'll be introducing next is completely different in content.

So this is my thesis, basically. Quick heads up though — our faculty's got kind of a weird system where you can't stick with the same lab once you move on to grad school. So the research I'm about to show you is honestly a totally different thing.
