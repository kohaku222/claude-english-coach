本日のリスニング教材です。2026年9月10日のセッションで扱った表現を収録しています。

今日のトピックは、ミーティングを丁寧にキャンセルする伝え方、そして相手からの提案に感謝しながら返事をする言い方などです。

それでは始めます。

まずは、急な予定変更を謝りながら伝える表現です。カジュアル、フォーマル、そしてよりネイティブらしい言い方を順番に聞いてみましょう。

Hey, sorry for the late message. I probably won't have much progress to show tomorrow — something personal came up after our last meeting and threw me off for a couple days. It's sorted now, so no worries, but could we skip tomorrow and pick back up next time?

Apologies for the late message. I wanted to flag that I won't have much progress to show tomorrow — something personal came up after our last meeting and set me back a couple of days. It's resolved now, so no need to worry, but could we skip tomorrow's meeting and reconvene next time?

ここがポイントです。

カジュアルな文では sorted や no worries のような軽い単語を使い、フォーマルな文では resolved や reconvene のような硬めの単語を使っています。同じ内容でも、相手との関係性に応じて単語の硬さを調整するのがネイティブの感覚です。

It's sorted now, so no worries. It's resolved now, so no need to worry.

続いて、よりくだけたネイティブらしい言い方です。

So sorry for texting this late. I just don't have much to show for tomorrow, honestly — something came up after we last met and knocked me off track for a few days. All good now, so no stress, but could we skip tomorrow and pick things up next time?

次は、相手が進捗なしでも会おうと言ってくれたことに対する返事です。

Thank you so much, that means a lot — I'll definitely come then! I'm currently filling out some paperwork at the IR office, so I might end up being a little late.

Thank you, I really appreciate that — I'll go ahead and come as planned. I'm currently at the IR office submitting some paperwork, so I may end up running a few minutes late.

ここがポイントです。

that means a lot と I really appreciate that は、どちらも感謝を強調する表現ですが、that means a lot の方がより感情がこもった温かい響きになります。フォーマルな場面では I really appreciate that の方が使いやすいです。

That means a lot. I really appreciate that.

最後に、よりくだけた言い方です。

Aw, thank you, that's really kind — I'll definitely swing by then! I'm actually at the IR office knocking out some paperwork right now, so I might be running a bit behind.
