まずは、味の濃さを表す語彙についてです。

This soup is really thick, the spoon can practically stand up in it.

It's not that thick, but the flavor is so dense it almost tastes like a reduction.

ここがポイントです。

thickはとろみや粘度の高さそのものを表し、denseは味や具が凝縮されている濃さを表します。両方とも日本語では濃いと訳されがちですが、視点が違います。

次は、can't getとdon't getの違いについてです。

I don't get the point of this meeting, why are we even discussing this?

No matter how many times he explains it, I just can't get it.

ここがポイントです。

don't getは今わかっていないという状態をそのまま表し、can't getは何度説明されても理解できないという限界のニュアンスを表します。

もう一度聞いてみましょう。

No matter how many times he explains it, I just can't get it.

次は、関係代名詞と関係副詞についてです。

The man who called me is my boss.

The book that I bought is great.

I know a guy whose sister is a famous singer.

The company which I work for is based in Tokyo.

This is the restaurant where we had our first date.

I remember the day when we first met.

That's the reason why I quit my job.

This is how I learned English.

次は、the first timeの使い方についてです。

This is the first time we're doing this.

This is my first time skiing.

This is the first time I've skied.

次は、abroadやhomeなど、前置詞なしで使う副詞についてです。

I want to travel abroad next year.

I'm going home now.

We traveled two hundred miles that day.

次は、ドリルで正しく言えていた表現の復習です。

That cafe I went to yesterday was pretty nice.

The key I was searching for was under my sofa.

The restaurant my friend recommended to me was amazing.

This is my first time working abroad.

Because I was tired, I decided to stay home today.

It might rain tomorrow, so you should bring an umbrella.

This is the reason why I started living alone.

The person we met last week turned out to be your colleague's friend.

I borrowed the book that he was talking about from the library.

次は、ドリルで間違えた表現の復習です。

ここは、is working forではなくworks forが自然でした。

The company she works for is based in Kyoto.

ここは、studying in Englishではなくstudying Englishが正しい形でした。

That's why I started studying English.

ここは、first challengeではなくthe first timeを使うところでした。

This is the first time I'm traveling abroad alone.

ここは、eventually didn't buyではなくended up not buying、recommended meではなくrecommended to meが正しい形でした。

I ended up not buying the shoes that the staff recommended to me.

ここは、go to and watchingではなくgo to watchが正しい形でした。

I ended up not going to watch the movie.

ここは、the first timeの後は現在形ではなく現在完了を使うところでした。

This is the first time I've worked abroad.

ここは、動詞を過去形にし、talking fromではなくtalking aboutを使うところでした。

I borrowed the book he was talking about from the library.

ここは、keeping the studying methodではなくsticking with the study methodが自然でした。

I ended up not sticking with the study method my teacher recommended to me.

ここは、trying to the marathonではなくattempting a marathonが正しい形でした。

This is the first time I'm attempting a marathon.

ここは、marathonの前に冠詞のaが必要でした。

This is my first time participating in a marathon.
